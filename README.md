# Grid Insights Platform

DESIGN DIRECTION:

The design must feel like a serious ENERGY / INFRASTRUCTURE / INDUSTRIAL technology product, not an AI-generated SaaS website.

DO NOT use:

- blue/purple gradients;

- neon AI aesthetics;

- excessive glowing effects;

- futuristic sci-fi visuals;

- generic glassmorphism;

- excessive rounded cards;

- generic AI illustrations;

- chatbot-style elements;

- stock-looking AI graphics;

- excessive animations.

DO NOT make it visually obvious that the website was generated by AI.

The visual identity must come from the ENERGY INDUSTRY itself.

Use:

- dark graphite / charcoal / off-white as the foundation;

- restrained industrial colors;

- green only for renewable energy;

- amber/orange for warnings and energy load;

- red only for critical risks;

- muted neutral colors for infrastructure;

- thin technical lines;

- grids;

- energy-flow diagrams;

- power-grid topology;

- Uzbekistan map;

- industrial data visualization.

The interface should resemble a combination of:

- modern energy control center;

- industrial engineering platform;

- power-grid monitoring system;

- high-end infrastructure visualization.

Typography should be clean, technical and professional.

Prioritize information hierarchy and visual storytelling over decorative UI.

The design should communicate:

ENERGY

INFRASTRUCTURE

RELIABILITY

EFFICIENCY

CONTROL

rather than:

AI

CHATBOT

FUTURE

SCI-FI

The AI should be represented through intelligence in the architecture and data flows, not through glowing "AI" visual effects.

Overall feeling:

"National energy infrastructure platform"

not

"AI startup landing page".
